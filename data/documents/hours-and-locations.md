three draw blood and collect samples. Reporting is done at the Hawthorne
laboratory, so turnaround times are the same whichever branch you visit.

## Holiday Closures

All branches are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence
Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, collection closes at 12:00 PM.
Reporting continues on those days, so samples already taken are still
released on time.

Holiday hours are posted at reception four weeks in advance.

## Parking and Transit

**Hawthorne.** Street parking on SE Hawthorne is metered with a two-hour
limit, enforced 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday to Saturday. A surface car park
behind the building, entered from SE 25th Avenue, is free for up to two
hours with a validation token from reception. TriMet bus lines 14 and 15
stop at SE Hawthorne & 24th, about 40 metres away.

**Beaverton.** Free car park on site, 30 spaces. Ten minutes' walk from
Beaverton Transit Center.

**Gresham.** Free street parking on NW Division. MAX Blue Line to Gresham
Central, then a five-minute walk.

## Accessibility

Beaverton and Gresham are single storey with step-free entrances.
Hawthorne has step-free access from the street and a lift to the second
floor.

All three branches have a seated collection station and can take samples
from a wheelchair without transfer. If you need assistance or extra time,
say so when booking and we will allocate a longer slot at no charge.
