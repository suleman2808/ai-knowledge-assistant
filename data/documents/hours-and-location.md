# Hours and Location

## Address

Riverbend Dental Care
2418 SE Hawthorne Boulevard, Suite 200
Portland, OR 97214

We are on the second floor of the Hawthorne Medical Building, above the
pharmacy. The building entrance is on SE Hawthorne Boulevard between 24th
and 25th Avenue. There is a lift to the left of the lobby as you enter.

The last appointment of each day is scheduled one hour before closing.
Wednesday evenings and Saturday mornings are our most requested slots and
are typically booked two to three weeks ahead.

## Holiday Closures

We are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
Thanksgiving Day and the day after, and December 24th through January 1st.

When a holiday falls on a weekend, the clinic observes the closure on the
adjacent Monday or Friday. Holiday closures are posted at reception and on
our voicemail four weeks in advance.

## Parking and Transit

Street parking on SE Hawthorne is metered, with a two-hour limit, and is
enforced from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday through Saturday.

A surface car park is available behind the building, accessed from SE 25th
Avenue. Patients may park there free of charge for up to three hours.
Collect a validation token from reception before you leave.

The building is served by TriMet bus lines 14 and 15. The nearest stop is
SE Hawthorne & 24th, roughly 40 metres from the entrance.

## Accessibility

The building has step-free access from the SE Hawthorne entrance and a lift
to the second floor. Two of our five treatment rooms are equipped for
wheelchair transfer.

If you use a mobility aid, or would like assistance from the lobby, please
tell us when booking so we can allocate an appropriate room and allow extra
time. There is no charge for this.
