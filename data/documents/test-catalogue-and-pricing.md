# Test Catalogue and Pricing

Self-pay prices, valid through December 2026. If you are insured, your
out-of-pocket cost is usually lower — see Insurance and Payment.

Turnaround is counted from when the sample reaches the Hawthorne
laboratory, not from when it was taken. Samples collected at Beaverton
and Gresham are couriered twice daily, at 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM.

"Fasting" means no food or drink except plain water for the stated
period. Medication is a separate question — see Preparation Instructions.

needs supervision over two hours, or for cultures requiring immediate
plating.

## What We Do Not Do

We do not perform imaging — X-ray, ultrasound, CT or MRI. We refer to
Portland Imaging Partners and send your details across at no charge.

We do not perform biopsies or any procedure requiring a clinician. We
process samples a clinician has taken.

We do not offer genetic or ancestry testing.
