# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can you tell me what my result means?

No. Not by telephone, not at the counter, not by email, and not even if
the pathologist who authorised it is standing there.

A result means something only alongside your symptoms, your history, your
medication and your examination. We have none of those. The same figure
is unremarkable in one person and needs urgent investigation in another.

Take your report to the doctor who requested it. If no doctor requested
it, any general practitioner can interpret it.

What we can tell you: whether the report is ready, what the test measures
in general, how the sample is collected, what the reference range on your
report is, and whether anything was flagged for a critical callback.

## Do I need to fast?

Only for lipid profile, fasting glucose, the glucose tolerance test, and
the panels containing them. Everything else, eat normally.

Fasting means no food or drink except plain water, for 8 to 12 hours.
Black coffee breaks the fast — it changes lipid and glucose readings.

## Can I drink water?

Yes, and you should. Dehydration makes veins harder to find and the draw
slower. Water does not affect any test we run.

## Do I need an appointment?

Not for most tests — walk in during collection hours. Book ahead for
fasting tests (so you are seen early), the glucose tolerance test, and
home collection.

## When will my report be ready?

It depends on the test; each one lists its turnaround in the catalogue.
Most routine blood tests are same day. Cultures take three to five days
because organisms grow at their own pace.

"Same day" means by 8:00 PM for samples taken before 2:00 PM.

## Can I get my report over the phone?

No. We cannot identify you reliably by voice, and a result given to the
wrong person cannot be withdrawn. Reports go by email, the patient
portal, WhatsApp on request, or collection in person with identification.

## Someone else is collecting my report. Is that allowed?

Yes, with their photographic identification and a signed note from you
naming them. Being a spouse or parent is not sufficient on its own.

## Do you come to my house?

Yes. $25 per visit, free above $150 of tests, in Portland, Beaverton,
Gresham, Tigard and Lake Oswego. Slots 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM, Monday to
Saturday. Book by 6:00 PM the day before on (503) 555-0146.

## Do I need a doctor's request?

Not for most tests — you can order directly from the catalogue. But
insurers rarely cover a test you ordered yourself, so if coverage matters,
see a doctor first.

## Does it hurt?

A sharp scratch for a second or two. Most people find the anticipation
worse than the event.

Tell the phlebotomist if you are anxious, if you have fainted before, or
if previous draws have been difficult. We will allow more time, collect
you lying down, and assign someone experienced. There is no charge, and
nobody will think it unusual.

## I'm frightened of needles.

Say so when you book and we will allocate a longer slot with our most
experienced phlebotomist, and collect you lying down from the start.

A large number of our patients say this. It is one of the commonest
reasons people put off tests their doctor asked for, which is a worse
outcome than a few uncomfortable minutes.

## Why do I bruise?

Usually because the cotton was lifted too early to check. Press firmly
for two full minutes without looking.

Bruising is also more likely on blood thinners, and in older skin. It
clears in three to five days. A warm compress helps after the first day.

## Can I have my test while pregnant?

Yes, and tell us you are pregnant — reference ranges differ for several
tests. Fasting for the stated periods is safe in pregnancy, but if you
have gestational diabetes your doctor should set the instructions.

## Will my employer see my results?

No. Where an employer pays for a pre-employment screen, they receive a
fit or unfit statement, not your results, unless you consent in writing
to anything more.

## Is HIV testing confidential?

Yes, absolutely. Results go to the person tested and to nobody else,
whoever paid. A positive result is telephoned by a pathologist
personally, with a free confirmatory test and a same-day specialist
referral.

## My result is outside the reference range. Should I worry?

Take it to a doctor — but know that ranges are defined so 95% of healthy
people fall inside them, which means one healthy person in twenty falls
outside by definition.

Slightly outside is common and often means nothing. We cannot tell you
which applies to you.

## Can I compare this with a test from another lab?

Not always. Different laboratories use different methods and different
reference ranges, especially for hormones and vitamin D. Your doctor may
ask you to repeat a test here rather than accept an outside report, and
that is not us drumming up business.

## How long do you keep my sample?

Seven days, then it is destroyed. If you doubt a result within that
window we can re-run the stored sample at no charge. After that a fresh
sample is needed.

## Do you do X-rays or scans?

No. We are a laboratory — blood, urine, stool, swabs. For imaging we
refer to Portland Imaging Partners and forward your details free of
charge.

## Do you do genetic or ancestry testing?

No.

## I had a test elsewhere and lost the report.

We cannot help with another laboratory's report; ask them. We can only
release reports for tests we performed.
