# Services and Pricing

All prices below are our self-pay rates, valid through December 2026. If you
are insured, your out-of-pocket cost will usually be lower. See the
Insurance and Payment document for how benefits are applied.

Prices are estimates. A definitive quote is given in writing after
examination, because the correct treatment cannot be determined from a
description of symptoms alone.

are usually seen every three to four months.

## What Is Not Offered Here

We do not provide general anaesthetic sedation, paediatric dentistry for
children under three, or jaw surgery. For these we refer to trusted
specialists in the Portland area and forward your records at no charge.
